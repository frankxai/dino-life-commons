## Identification

Two long brow horns, a shorter nasal horn, and a large bony frill define the skull. Body is robust and quadrupedal with a beaked mouth suited to tough plant processing.

## Ecology

Among the most abundant large dinosaurs in Hell Creek–equivalent assemblages. Horns and frill are discussed in contexts of defense, species recognition, and display — interpretations evolve with new specimens.
