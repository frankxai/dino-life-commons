## Identification

Ankylosaurus is built like a biological tank: a broad armored body, short limbs, a wide beaked skull, and a stiffened tail ending in a heavy bony club. Osteoderms form a mosaic of plates and spikes across the back and flanks. It is **not** a bipedal theropod; posture is quadrupedal and the silhouette is low and wide.

## Ecology and behavior

Fossil associations place Ankylosaurus in latest Cretaceous coastal plain ecosystems of western North America. Armor and the tail club are widely interpreted as anti-predator defenses in habitats shared with large theropods such as *Tyrannosaurus*. Feeding ecology is low-browse herbivory; exact diet plants remain model-dependent.
