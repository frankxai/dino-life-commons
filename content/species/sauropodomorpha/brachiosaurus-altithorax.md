## Identification

Unlike many sauropods with more horizontal necks, brachiosaurids are reconstructed as high browsers: longer forelimbs, steeply rising neck, and a relatively small head. Absolute mass estimates vary widely by method.

## Ecology

Interpreted as a tall plant browser in Jurassic forested floodplain settings. Herd structure, growth rates, and neck posture remain active research areas.
