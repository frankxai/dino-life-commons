## Identification

*T. rex* is a large bipedal theropod with a deep skull, serrated teeth, robust hind limbs, reduced arms, and a long balancing tail. Modern reconstructions emphasize scientific proportions over mid-century movie caricature.

## Ecology

Often interpreted as an apex predator and/or opportunistic scavenger in latest Cretaceous ecosystems of western North America. Co-occurs with large herbivores including *Triceratops* and *Ankylosaurus*. Behavioral details remain active research topics.
